eBay UK Private Sellers Pay 0% Final Value Fees — The Catch

Here is a fee fact most UK sellers do not know: eBay charges no final value fees and no regulatory operating fees to UK-based private sellers. On a £48 order, a private seller pays £0 in final value fees. The only exceptions are motors listings (Cars, Motorcycles & Vehicles), fees above 300 listings a month (35p per extra listing), optional listing upgrades, and a 3% international fee on delivery to an overseas address. For a casual seller clearing out a wardrobe or selling a few handmade items, eBay is effectively free at the point of sale.

Business sellers face a very different bill. eBay UK charges business sellers a final value fee that varies by category — around 10.9% in many categories, 9.9% for Business and Coins, 8.9% for Cameras and Mobiles, with lower tiered rates above thresholds — plus a per-order fee of £0.30 on orders of £10 or less and £0.40 on orders over £10. On a £48 order in a 10.9% category, that is about £5.23 plus £0.40 — roughly £5.63, or nearly 12% of the sale.

Compare that with Etsy for a UK seller. Etsy charges a US$0.20 listing fee per listing, a 6.5% transaction fee on the price plus shipping charged, payment processing of 4% + £0.20, and a regulatory operating fee of 0.48% of the price plus shipping. On the same £48 order, that is £3.12 in transaction fees, £2.12 in payment processing and £0.23 in regulatory fees — about £5.47 before the listing fee. And your own store? Shopify's Basic plan in the UK is £25 a month with online card rates of 2% + 25p per order — about £1.21 on a £48 order, plus the monthly plan spread across your orders.

That is the real lesson. The same platform can be free for one seller and 12% for another, depending on whether eBay classes you as private or business — and the moment you start selling regularly, the private-seller deal stops applying.
